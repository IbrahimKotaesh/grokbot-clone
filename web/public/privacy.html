Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Fino gives you a team of AI assistants ("bots") that can chat, use connected apps and work on a cloud computer for you. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal information and we do not show ads.

Information we collect

  • Account details. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture.
  • Your content. Messages, voice messages, photos and files you send to your bots, the replies they write, the memories and skills they keep, and the routines and tasks you set up.
  • Model connections. If you connect your own AI account or API key (for example ChatGPT, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Groq or xAI), we store the API key encrypted, or the sign-in session that provider issues, so your bots can use it. We never see your ChatGPT or Claude password.
  • Connected apps. If you connect apps such as Gmail, Google Calendar or Slack, the connection is managed by our integration provider and used only when a bot acts for you. Sensitive actions ask for your approval first.
  • Cloud computer. On plans that include one, your bots work on a private cloud computer. Files and browser sessions on it belong to your account.
  • Subscription status. Apple processes payments. We receive your plan and renewal status, not your payment card.
  • Basic usage. Plan limits such as the number of bots and computer hours used, and technical logs needed to keep the service running and secure.

How we use it

  • To run your bots and show you their work.
  • To enforce plan limits and manage your subscription.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To send notifications you turn on.

We do not use your content to train our own models.

Services that process your data

To do the work you ask for, parts of your content are sent to these providers, under their own privacy terms:

  • AI model providers you choose for each bot: OpenAI (ChatGPT), Anthropic (Claude), OpenRouter, Groq or xAI. Voice messages and calls are transcribed and spoken by xAI.
  • Badr's own models (Badr 5.1 and Badr 4.8) are open-source models (from DeepSeek) that Badr runs through OpenRouter on independent hosting companies, which may be located in the United States, Europe or Asia (including China). We only use hosts that do not store your prompts or use them for training. Fino did not train these models.
  • Composio, for connections to apps you link.
  • Orgo, which hosts cloud computers.
  • Apple and RevenueCat, for subscriptions.
  • Google, for sign-in.

Storage and security

Your data is stored on our servers. API keys are encrypted at rest, connections use HTTPS, and each user's cloud computer and model sign-ins are kept separate from other users.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete individual memories, conversations and bots in the app, and you can delete your whole account in Account → Delete account. Deleting your account removes your bots, conversations, memories, files, keys and model sign-ins from our servers. Backups are overwritten within 30 days. Cancel your subscription separately in your App Store settings.

Your choices

  • Disconnect any model or app at any time in the app.
  • Turn notifications and microphone access on or off in iPhone Settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data or for corrections through the support page.

Children

Fino is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the date above. Material changes will also be shown in the app.
