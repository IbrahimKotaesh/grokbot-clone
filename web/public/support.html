Common questions

How do I connect ChatGPT or Claude?

Open Account → AI models. Tap Sign in with ChatGPT to use your ChatGPT subscription, or add an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Groq or xAI. To use your Claude subscription, open Account → Claude Code (Pro plan and up) and sign in there.

A bot says its model is out of usage.

Your model provider has a usage limit. If you connect more than one model, Fino switches to another automatically. Otherwise, wait for your limit to reset or add another model in AI models.

How do I change or cancel my plan?

Upgrade in Account → Plan. To cancel, open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Fino. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

I bought a plan on another device.

Open Account → Plan and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID.

I can't see my bot's computer.

Computer use is part of the Pro and Max plans. The computer sleeps when nobody uses it. Open the bot's computer screen and tap Wake computer.

How do I delete my account?

Open Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your bots, conversations, memories, files and connected keys. Cancel any subscription in your App Store settings first.
