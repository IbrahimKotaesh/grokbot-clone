# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Anyone (public launch). People who want an AI team that does real work for them: reading email, researching, planning their day, and operating a computer and browser on their behalf. They arrive on the public site, sign in with Google, and use Fino on the web or in the Mac app.

## Product Purpose
Fino is a workspace of AI bots. Each bot has a name, a look, its own memory and a job. You give a bot a task in chat and follow its work. Success: a visitor understands this in seconds, signs in, and gets their first bot working.

## Positioning
Bots that don't just answer: they use a real computer and browser (you watch the cursor move and can take over), and they connect to your apps (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets, Slack, Notion).

## Operating Context
- Web app at the site root after sign-in (Google OAuth; an optional access-code gate exists per installation).
- Mac app (desktop/macos) wraps the web workspace and adds a "Fino Browser" side panel the bot controls with a visible cursor.
- iPhone app exists (TestFlight), not yet public.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Bots: roles, memories, routines/recurring tasks, group chats with @mentions, voice.
- AI brains: user's ChatGPT or Claude subscription, or Fino's included models (Qwen / GLM / DeepSeek). Never Gemini.
- Write actions on connected apps pause for approval.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Fino. Mascot: blue character `web/public/characters/fino-brand-blue.png`. Bot avatar characters in `web/public/characters/`.
- Primary blue #235bd6. Fonts IBM Plex Sans (+ Tajawal for Arabic).
- Voice: simple, warm, plain words; no jargon, no unnecessary menus or complexity.
- Page language: English.

## Evidence on Hand
Real product screenshots in the side-session notes (Fino Browser on Indeed). No testimonials, customer logos, user counts, or pricing to cite: do not invent them.

## Product Principles
1. Simple first: one obvious next step.
2. Show the bots doing real work, not abstract AI claims.
3. You stay in control: watch, approve, take over.
