## App Review information

**Sign-in:** Use **Sign in with Apple** on the first screen (no demo account needed). Google sign-in is also available.

**Notes for the reviewer:**
Fino is an AI assistant app. After signing in, a short onboarding creates a starter team of bots. Open any bot and send a message to see it work. New accounts start on the Free plan. Subscriptions (Starter, Pro, Max) are auto-renewable and can be bought from Account → Plan; Restore Purchases is on the same screen. Computer use is part of Pro and Max. Users can delete their account in Account → Delete account.

**Age rating:** answer that the app includes unrestricted web access (bots can browse the web) and AI-generated content. This makes the rating 18+.

**App Privacy (data collected, linked to the user, not used for tracking):** contact info (name, email), user content (messages, photos, audio, files), identifiers (user ID), purchases. No tracking, no ads.
