## App Review information

**Sign-in:** Use **Sign in with Apple** on the first screen (no demo account needed). Google sign-in is also available.

**Notes for the reviewer:**
Badr is an AI assistant app. After signing in, a short onboarding sets up a starter team. Open Badr or any teammate and send a message to see it work; the phone button in a chat starts a voice call. New accounts can try any plan free for one day. Subscriptions (Starter, Pro, Max) are auto-renewable and can be bought from Account → Plan; Restore Purchases is on the same screen. Users can delete their account in Account → Delete account.

**Age rating:** answer that the app includes unrestricted web access (teammates can browse the web) and AI-generated content.

**App Privacy (data collected, linked to the user, not used for tracking):** contact info (name, email), user content (messages, photos, audio, files), identifiers (user ID), purchases. No tracking, no ads.
